🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: Bhuvan portal is slow
→ Solution: Try mirror https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/

Problem: Can't download from USGS DAAC
→ Solution: Use Google Earth Engine or contact daac-services@ornl.gov

Problem: Shapefile incomplete (missing .dbf or .shx)
→ Solution: Download again, ensure all 4 files (.shp/.dbf/.shx/.prj) present

Problem: Wrong coordinates/region
→ Solution: Verify AOI bounds: Lat 24.0-25.0°N, Lon 93.5-94.5°E

Problem: Network issues during download
→ Solution: Use browser download or retry with alternate mirror
